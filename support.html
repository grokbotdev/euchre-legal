A hand

You sit South. Your partner sits across the table. Order the turned card up, name a trump, or pass. The dealer must name a suit if everyone passes twice. Hints, when they are on, suggest one bid or one card. House rules for the next game are on the rules screen.

Ads

A banner sits at the bottom of the table on a phone. A full-screen ad can appear after every other finished game, and it has a close button. It does not appear in the middle of a hand. The short video is optional and does not change the score.

Remove ads

Remove ads is a one-time purchase under $5. It removes the banner and the full-screen ads. On the rules screen, Restore purchases puts that purchase back on a new phone signed in to the same store account. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Privacy

How ads, consent, and on-device game data are handled is written at https://grokbotdev.github.io/euchre-legal/privacy.html.
