Euchre: Classic Cards - Privacy Policy

Who sells this app

Euchre is sold by SBM Syndicate LLC. This policy covers the iOS and Android apps and the public web pages at this site.

Game data stays on the device

Settings, statistics, the daily record, and the hand on the table are stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no upload of that game data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising and AdMob

Phone builds show ads through Google AdMob. A banner stays at the bottom of the table. A full-screen ad can appear after every other finished game, and you can close it. A short video plays only if you choose to watch it, and watching it does not change the hand. Google may receive device identifiers, including the advertising ID, to deliver and measure those ads.

Ad consent

Before ads are personalized, the app can show two prompts. On iPhone, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the app may track you. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows an ad consent form where the law requires one. If you decline, the game still plays. The web build does not show these ads or these prompts.

Purchases

Remove ads is a one-time purchase under $5, processed by Apple or Google. The seller does not receive your card number. The store tells the app whether you own that purchase so the banner and the full-screen ads can stay off. You can restore the purchase from the rules screen on a phone. The reward video stays optional.

Children

Euchre is not directed at children under 13, and it is not part of a kids program.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the support page: https://grokbotdev.github.io/euchre-legal/support.html.
